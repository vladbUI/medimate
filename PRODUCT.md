# MediMate
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User requested React, Vite, Tailwind, Dexie and PDF extraction.
## Users and purpose
A MedTech student studying transes privately on phone and laptop. Extract every page incrementally, review source-linked drafts, then study with flashcards, quizzes and timed moving exams.
## Constraints
Local mode first. Optional AI disabled until securely configured; PHP 500 monthly ceiling. Editable placeholder personalization. Export/import for device transfers. No medical facts fabricated as verified content.
## Brand commitments
User approved calm notebook workspace, warm white and plum accents, built directly.
